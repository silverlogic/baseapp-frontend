Card holds (`baseapp_payments`): the client half for a project's checkout mutation that places a card hold through `CardHoldPaymentInputMixin` / `CardHoldPayloadMixin`. New `@baseapp-frontend/components/payments/common` subpath with `useCardHoldPlacement` (and `placeCardHold` outside React): it sends the card with a client-generated attempt id, runs 3D Secure on `requires_action` (for a PaymentIntent or a SetupIntent) and resends `paymentIntentId` / `setupIntentId` under the same attempt id, resends `in_progress` with exponential backoff, resends a `changed` answer once under a new attempt id, and resolves one outcome per status (`placed`, `scheduled` with `holdOn`, `card_saved`, `charge_now`, `declined`, `changed`, `amount_changed`, `refused`, `in_progress`, plus `authentication_failed`, `error`, and `cancelled` after unmount). The attempt id is renewed only after `declined`, `changed` and `refused`. Outcome payloads never carry `clientSecret`. A second `place()` while one runs joins it; unmounting aborts the run (no further 3D Secure, resend or backoff) and `onOutcome` is not called after unmount. `placeCardHold` takes an `AbortSignal` and `onAttemptIdChange`. The Stripe call is injected, so the hook is platform-neutral; `payments/web` adds `useStripeCardHoldPlacement` and `createStripeJsCardHoldAuthenticator` (Stripe.js `handleNextAction`). Also in `payments/web`: `CardHoldNotice` (tells the customer the card is held now, will be held on a date, is saved and charged on the date, or is charged now; it has no failure copy), `CardHoldStatusLabel` (a chip from the `CardHoldStatusLabel_cardHold` fragment; customers never see "failed") and `CardHoldStaffActions` (failure reason plus Keep / Cancel with a confirmation, through the new `cardHoldKeep` / `cardHoldCancel` mutation hooks). `schema.graphql` gains the `CardHold` type and the two mutations. Copy is English and replaceable through `messages` / `getLabel` props.
