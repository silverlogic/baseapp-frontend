Card holds: staff can retry a failed hold or mark a partially paid one paid. `CardHoldStaffNotice` renders as an error `Alert` for a failed hold (info once kept) and a warning `Alert` for a partially paid one, with the actions inside. It gains a "Retry hold" action beside Keep and Cancel, shown for a failed hold that wasn't kept while `retriesLeft > 0`, with an "N retries left" line (and, at zero, a line saying only Keep or Cancel remain). Its confirmation says the same saved card is tried again now, off-session. It commits the new `useCardHoldRetryMutation` (`cardHoldRetry`), and `onDecided('retry')` fires only when the backend accepted it; a retry that fails again leaves the failed panel with one retry fewer, and one that comes back authorized, or scheduled (the hold date moved into the future, so the hold is placed later), removes it. The component now renders nothing unless the new `CardHold.canDecide` is true (the backend sets it only for a viewer granted `change_cardhold`; false for customers), which hides both the failed and the partially paid panels. The failed panel, kept notice included, also goes once the hold is paid (a kept failed hold that was then charged stays `failed`). For a `partially_paid` hold it now offers "Mark paid" (`useCardHoldMarkPaidMutation`, `cardHoldMarkPaid`; `onDecided('markPaid')`). `CardHoldStaffDecision` gains `retry` and `markPaid`, and `messages` takes the new copy (`retriesLeft` is a function of the count). New `CARD_HOLD_PAYMENT_STATUS` and `CardHoldPaymentStatus`. `CardHoldStaffNotice_cardHold` adds `retriesLeft`, `paymentStatus` and `canDecide`, and `schema.graphql` gains `CardHold.paymentStatus`, `CardHold.retriesLeft`, `CardHold.canDecide`, `cardHoldRetry` and `cardHoldMarkPaid`; this needs a backend with BA-3483, the card hold retry and `CardHold.canDecide`.
